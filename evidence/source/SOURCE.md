# Source paper

The barrier results in this repository are read against

> S. Shelah, *Was Sierpinski right? I*, Israel Journal of Mathematics 62 (1988), 355-380.
> Shelah archive number 289.

The paper itself is **not redistributed here**. It is the author's work, not ours, and this
repository's Apache-2.0 license does not extend to it. Shelah's archive makes his papers
available at https://shelah.logic.at/ and the paper is indexed there under number 289.

An earlier commit in this repository did include a copy of the PDF and a text extraction of it.
Both were removed on 2026-09-10. Nothing in this repository cited them.
